## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated chill of deep-space research, computational laboratories, and precision flight telemetry. It bridges two worlds: the mathematical rigor of orbital mechanics and the humanistic cadence of editorial science journals. 

The aesthetic is rooted in minimal brutalism and telemetry-driven tactical interfaces. Visual mass is reduced to absolute essentials: deep pitch-black voids, hairline vector coordinate lines, reticles, micro-etched telemetry readouts, and high-contrast editorial typography. Rather than generic cyberpunk neon clutter, the system is disciplined, clinical, and quiet—evoking the tension of a dark laboratory observation deck tracking sub-millisecond trajectories.

Key visual attributes:
- **Atmospheric Abyss:** Absolute deep matte blacks layered with subtle sub-surface charcoal panels.
- **Micro-Precision HUD:** 1px hairline grids, coordinate brackets, crosshair reticles, and subtle teal vector traces.
- **Editorial Contrast:** High-contrast serif headlines colliding against ultra-technical monospaced readouts and data tables.
- **Status Luminescence:** Vibrant cyan and neon teal glows applied sparingly to denote active velocity, calibration states, and focal targets, contrasted by coral alerts.

## Colors

The palette operates under an uncompromising dark mode paradigm designed to eliminate eye fatigue in low-light operational environments while maintaining pinpoint data legibility.

- **Primary (`#2dd4bf`):** Active telemetry cyan. Reserved for live vectors, primary actions, active radar bearings, and calibrated metrics.
- **Secondary (`#38bdf8`):** Celestial electric blue. Used for secondary sensor indicators, navigational nodes, and status highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#fb923c`):** Precision coral/orange. Designates high-priority warnings, critical thresholds, target locks, and friction warnings.
- **Neutral (`#0a0d0e`):** The baseline carbon floor. Serves as the deep canvas background across all viewport surfaces.
- **Named Colors:**
  - `void-black` (`#050708`): Deepest recessive wells, inset monitors, and simulation stages.
  - `surface-frame` (`#131c20`): Panel backgrounds, modular telemetry blocks, and card surfaces.
  - `surface-border` (`#1f2937`): Hairline structural containment borders and graticules.
  - `alert-coral` (`#f87171`): Abort signals, boundary collisions, and failure states.
  - `status-gold` (`#facc15`): Calibration targets, optimal milestones, and operational badges.

Ensure chromatic accents are applied strictly to dynamic data points, reticles, or status pings; static content remains muted monochrome to preserve situational awareness.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates tension through deliberate contrast:
1. **The Editorial Voice (`Playfair Display`):** Used exclusively for high-level titles, introspective section headers, and philosophical questions. It introduces human warmth, historical significance, and a high-end publication tone amidst cold laboratory instruments.
2. **The Instrument Readout (`JetBrains Mono`):** Powers all system statuses, tabular data, HUD bearings, keyboard shortcut tags, and micro-labels. Characters are strictly rendered in uppercase for micro-labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) with generous letter-spacing to ensure absolute legibility even at 9px.
3. **The Clear Conduit (`Inter`):** Assigned to mission briefings, technical descriptions, and explanatory documentation to guarantee neutral clarity without competing with the display serif or telemetry mono.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a modular instrumentation grid engineered to resemble spacecraft telemetry panels and lab terminals.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column dynamic grid on desktop (`>1024px`) with `1.5rem` gutters and outer canvas margins of `3rem`. Collapses to a 6-column grid on tablet (`768px - 1023px`) and a 4-column compact stack on mobile (`<768px`).
- **Telemetry Shell:** Operational layouts feature fixed edge-anchored HUD bars (top protocol bar, bottom command strip) with a dynamic viewport stage in the center. Data cards dock to lateral sidebars spanning 3 to 4 columns.
- **Rhythm & Alignment:** Spacing uses a modular 4px/8px base cadence. Micro metrics use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) gaps to simulate compact avionics consoles, while macro sections breathe through `space-xl` and `space-2xl` expanses to establish architectural clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids soft diffusion shadows and skeuomorphic blur gradients, relying instead on structural planar boundaries and pinpoint luminescence:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Backdrop Void):* `#050708` or `#0a0d0e`. Non-interactive backdrop and deep sensor viewport.
  - *Tier 1 (Instrumentation Plates):* `#131c20` with `1px solid #1f2937` perimeter bounding. Applied to data boxes, telemetry modules, and briefing cards.
  - *Tier 2 (Floating Overlays & Menus):* `#0a0d0e` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and crisp `#2dd4bf26` hairline perimeter borders.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Hairlines:** Depth is articulated via 1px crisp stroke borders. Structural framing uses `#1f2937`. Active or selected modules shift to `#2dd4bf` (cyan) or `#fb923c` (coral) borders.
- **Target Glow & Luminescence:** Focal elements (such as reticles, active ship arrows, or active sliders) emit a restrained localized bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.35)`.
- **HUD Reticle Corners:** Telemetry containers use decorative hairline corner markers (`+` crosshairs or `L`-brackets) positioned outside the border frame to reinforce tactical hardware elevation.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular is strictly sharp and monolithic (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Corners:** 0px border radius across all primary containers, input panels, data modules, buttons, and HUD badges. This reinforces industrial CNC-machined instruments, avionics readouts, and tactical lab displays.
- **Beveled Chamfers:** For high-level mode toggles or simulation boundaries, containers may employ 45-degree angled cut corners (4px to 6px chamfer via CSS `clip-path: polygon(...)`) to express an authentic sci-fi flight-deck aesthetic.
- **Dividers & Crosshairs:** Section dividers are razor-thin 1px solid lines with terminal registration dots or subtle coordinate ticks along their length.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#2dd4bf` surface with `#050708` bold monospace text (`label-md`). Hover transitions to pure white text with a soft teal luminescence glow. Sharp 0px corners.
- **Ghost/HUD Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #1f2937`, white or `#2dd4bf` label. Hover states shift the border to `#2dd4bf` with a faint 6% cyan background fill.
- **Keybind Indicators:** Secondary action triggers incorporate monospaced shortcut caps (e.g., `[ SPACE ]`, `[ R ]`) formatted in `label-sm` with a distinct `#1f2937` border frame.

### Data Cards & Telemetry Blocks
- Grounded on `#131c20` with a 1px `#1f2937` outer border.
- Headers are partitioned by a horizontal 1px hairline stroke. The top-left features `label-sm` tracking tags (e.g., `PROTOCOL // TELEMETRY`) and top-right features coordinate brackets or status indicators.
- Metrics are anchored by `telemetry-display` numerals in `JetBrains Mono` accompanied by unit superscripts in teal or coral.

### Inputs & Sliders
- **Text Inputs:** Recessed `#050708` background with a crisp `#1f2937` border. Monospaced input values. Active/focus state converts the border to `#2dd4bf` without standard OS outline halos.
- **Range & Scrub Bars:** 2px thin track in `#1f2937`. Active fill drawn in `#2dd4bf` or `#facc15`. Sliders feature 2px x 12px vertical reticle bars rather than rounded thumbs.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- Rectangular pill alternative: 0px sharp badges. Monospaced uppercase text (`label-sm`).
- Preceded by a 4px pulsing square or diamond status indicator (teal for nominal, gold for standby, coral for ranked/hazard).

### HUD Reticles & Graphs
- Sparklines and vector tunnels feature ultra-light 1px or 1.5px SVG strokes.
- Axes utilize micro-dashes with numeric pitch bearings (e.g., `315°`, `045°`) styled in `label-sm`.
